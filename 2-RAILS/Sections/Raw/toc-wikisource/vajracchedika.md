The thirteen inline headings of the Derge Kangyur text on its Wikisource Index page (revision 1197220). The Index's title heading is the work's title, not a node. Rows 1–4 of the display (title, Sanskrit and Tibetan titles, homage) precede node 1 and take ids `^0-n`.

The headings are numbered 1–13 on the page with no nesting by number (their `===`/`====` levels alternate without a pattern), so they are kept flat.

Rows 38 and 317 are split (D7): on the proofread page the heading stands after the question དེ་ཅིའི་ཕྱིར་ཞེ་ན། and before the answer. The row's Sanskrit segment is shown with part 2 (it renders the sentence the heading opens), so the Sanskrit heading stands before that segment. The Chinese (Kumārajīva) is paired with the display rows one for one; its rows 38 and 317 hold the same question and answer (何以故？ / 所以者何？ + the answer) and are split at the same place (D7), each part showing the Tibetan part it renders.
