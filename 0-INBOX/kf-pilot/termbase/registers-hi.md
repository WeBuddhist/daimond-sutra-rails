# Registers for the Diamond Sutra Hindi versions (Devanagari)

## Academic (शैक्षणिक संस्करण)
- Readers: Hindi-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern standard Hindi in Devanagari; precise and faithful.
- Terminology: the established Sanskrit (tatsama) Buddhist terms used in Hindi Buddhist scholarship (तथागत, भगवान, बोधिसत्त्व महासत्त्व, अनुत्तर सम्यक्सम्बोधि, दान, पुण्य, प्रज्ञापारमिता, स्रोतापन्न, अर्हत्); proper names in their Sanskrit form (सुभूति, श्रावस्ती, जेतवन, दीपंकर). Avoid Hindu-devotional or Urdu-Persian substitutes for technical terms.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (बच्चों का संस्करण)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Hindi.
- Simple everyday Hindi; short common words. A technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names kept (बुद्ध, सुभूति, श्रावस्ती); technical Sanskrit compounds replaced by plain phrases.
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
