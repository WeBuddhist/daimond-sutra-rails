# Registers for the Diamond Sutra Chinese versions (Traditional characters)

## Academic (學術版)
- Readers: Chinese-reading students, practitioners and scholars of Buddhist texts.
- Modern written Chinese (白話書面語), Traditional characters, full-width punctuation.
- Terminology: use the established Chinese Buddhist term where one exists, preferring Kumārajīva's Diamond Sūtra usage (須菩提, 如來, 世尊, 菩薩摩訶薩, 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提, 布施, 福德, 我相) — attested in the Kumārajīva examples given. Where the Tibetan distinguishes something Kumārajīva's wording blurs (e.g. the two senses of ཆོས), choose the established term that keeps the distinction (法 / 諸法, 佛法), following the English glossary's sense split.
- One locked rendering per term (per sense).

## Children's (兒童版)
- Readers: children aged 8–12 reading Chinese.
- Plain everyday modern Chinese, Traditional characters. Short words a child knows; a technical idea becomes a simple phrase that keeps the meaning, never a different idea.
- Names: the common Chinese name (佛陀, 須菩提, 舍衛城); no transliterated technical Sanskrit terms (not 阿耨多羅三藐三菩提 — say it simply).
- One rendering per term (per sense), used every time.
