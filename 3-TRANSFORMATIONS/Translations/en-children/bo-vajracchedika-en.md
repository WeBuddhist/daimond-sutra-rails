## The Setting ^1-0

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-1]]

This is what I heard one time. ^1-1

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-2]]

The Blessed One was staying in Shravasti at the Jeta Grove, the park of Anathapindada. A great community of one thousand two hundred and fifty monks was there. Many great awakening heroes were with him too. ^1-2

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-3]]

In the morning, the Blessed One put on his lower robe and his teaching robe. He picked up his alms bowl. He walked into the great city of Shravasti for his alms food. ^1-3

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-4]]

The Blessed One went to the great city of Shravasti for alms food. He ate his meal and finished eating. ^1-4

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-5]]

He finished eating his alms food. He put away his alms bowl and teaching robe. He washed his feet and sat cross-legged on his seat. He sat up straight with careful attention, ready to pay full attention. ^1-5

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-6]]

Then many monks went over to where the Blessed One was. They walked right up to him. ^1-6
