## Setting ^1-0

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-1]]

Thus did I hear at one time. ^1-1

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-2]]

The Blessed One was dwelling in Śrāvastī at Jetavana, the park of Anāthapiṇḍada, together with a great community of one thousand two hundred and fifty monks, and very many bodhisattva great beings. ^1-2

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-3]]

Then, in the morning time, the Blessed One put on his lower robe and Dharma robe, took up his alms bowl, and went to the great city of Śrāvastī for alms. ^1-3

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-4]]

Then the Blessed One, having gone to the great city of Śrāvastī for alms, partook of his meal and completed the action of eating, ^1-4

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-5]]

Having given up later alms, he set aside his alms bowl and Dharma robe, washed his feet, sat cross-legged on the arranged seat, straightened his body, and remained, having established mindfulness to the fore. ^1-5

![[bo-vajracchedika#^1-6]]

Then many monks went to where the Blessed One was, and having approached him, ^1-6
